Our Wings

Each wing is designed to nurture specific talents and skills, creating well-rounded leaders for tomorrow's Bharat.

01

Ideation

Motto

Think. Create. Innovate.

Why Join?

Be the architect of new ideas and shape the future through creativity.

What It's About

Brainstorming sessions, innovation challenges, and creative problem-solving.

Requirements

Creative mindset, willingness to think outside the box, and passion for innovation.

FAQ

Q: Do I need prior experience?
A: No, just a curious mind!

02

Documentation

Motto

Preserve. Record. Inspire.

Why Join?

Master the art of storytelling and preserve the legacy of IYC.

What It's About

Writing, content creation, documentation, and archival work.

Requirements

Good writing skills, attention to detail, and passion for documentation.

FAQ

Q: What kind of documentation work?
A: Reports, newsletters, social media content, and archival.

03

Social Media

Motto

Connect. Engage. Inspire.

Why Join?

Amplify the IYC voice and build a digital community of changemakers.

What It's About

Content creation, social media management, campaigns, and digital outreach.

Requirements

Creativity, basic knowledge of social media platforms, and passion for digital storytelling.

FAQ

Q: Do I need to know graphic design?
A: Not mandatory, but willingness to learn is a plus!

04

Information Technology

Motto

Innovate. Code. Transform.

Why Join?

Build the digital backbone of IYC and drive technological innovation.

What It's About

Website development, app creation, tech support, and digital solutions.

Requirements

Basic coding knowledge (HTML/CSS/JS), problem-solving skills, and tech enthusiasm.

FAQ

Q: What programming languages?
A: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and we also explore Python!

05

Skill Development

Motto

Empower. Equip. Elevate.

Why Join?

Help others discover their potential and build essential life skills.

What It's About

Workshops, training sessions, skill-based programs, and personal development.

Requirements

Passion for teaching, good communication skills, and leadership mindset.

FAQ

Q: What skills do we teach?
A: Communication, leadership, public speaking, and more!

06

Events

Motto

Plan. Execute. Celebrate.

Why Join?

Create unforgettable experiences and bring the IYC community together.

What It's About

Event planning, coordination, logistics, and community engagement activities.

Requirements

Organizational skills, creativity, and ability to work in a team.

FAQ

Q: What types of events?
A: Workshops, seminars, cultural events, and community outreach.

07

Model United Nations

Motto

Think. Debate. Diplomacy.

Why Join?

Develop diplomatic skills and represent Bharat on global platforms.

What It's About

MUN conferences, diplomacy training, debate sessions, and global affairs discussions.

Requirements

Interest in international relations, debating skills, and research mindset.

FAQ

Q: Do I need MUN experience?
A: No, we train you from the ground up!

08

Fine Arts

Motto

Create. Express. Inspire.

Why Join?

Unleash your creativity and bring beauty to the IYC community.

What It's About

Painting, sculpture, digital art, exhibitions, and artistic expression.

Requirements

Passion for art, creativity, and willingness to experiment with different mediums.

FAQ

Q: Do I need professional training?
A: No, we welcome artists of all levels!

09

Innovation

Motto

Dream. Build. Disrupt.

Why Join?

Transform ideas into reality and shape the future through innovation.

What It's About

Prototyping, hackathons, innovation challenges, and product development.

Requirements

Creative thinking, problem-solving skills, and passion for building things.

FAQ

Q: What kind of projects?
A: Tech solutions, social innovations, and creative products.

10

Literary

Motto

Write. Read. Inspire.

Why Join?

Express your thoughts and build a literary culture in IYC.

What It's About

Creative writing, poetry, book reviews, literary events, and publications.

Requirements

Love for words, reading habits, and passion for creative expression.

FAQ

Q: Do I need to be a writer?
A: Just a love for literature is enough!

11

Commerce

Motto

Learn. Grow. Trade.

Why Join?

Develop business acumen and financial literacy for the future.

What It's About

Business studies, financial literacy, economics discussions, and entrepreneurship.

Requirements

Interest in commerce, business, and financial literacy.

FAQ

Q: Do I need a commerce background?
A: No, just curiosity about business and finance!

12

Humanities

Motto

Question. Understand. Connect.

Why Join?

Explore human society, culture, and the world around us.

What It's About

Social studies, philosophy, history, psychology, and cultural discussions.

Requirements

Curiosity about society, critical thinking, and love for learning.

FAQ

Q: What topics do we cover?
A: Philosophy, sociology, history, and contemporary issues.

13

Environmental Wellness

Motto

Protect. Sustain. Thrive.

Why Join?

Be a guardian of our planet and promote environmental awareness.

What It's About

Sustainability, environmental awareness, green initiatives, and wellness.

Requirements

Passion for environment, sustainability mindset, and action-oriented approach.

FAQ

Q: What kind of activities?
A: Plantation drives, cleanups, awareness campaigns, and more.

14

Legal

Motto

Know. Protect. Lead.

Why Join?

Understand the law and become a voice for justice in society.

What It's About

Legal awareness, constitutional values, rights and duties, and legal advocacy.

Requirements

Interest in law, justice, and social advocacy.

FAQ

Q: Do I need a law degree?
A: No, just passion for justice and legal awareness!
