President, Ignite Youth Community

Pranav Suchindran

President, Ignite Youth Community (IYC)

Diplomatic Authoritative Trail Blazer | National Award Winner | Youth Leader

Pranav Suchindran is a young nation-builder, diplomatic thinker, researcher, public speaker, and youth leader whose work is anchored in the timeless ideal of Rashtra Devo Bhava — the Nation as the highest duty.

As the President of Ignite Youth Community (IYC), Pranav is shaping a youth movement founded on a simple conviction: an empowered, knowledgeable, and responsible youth is the foundation of a developed nation. His vision centres on knowledge sharing, mentorship, peer-to-peer learning, leadership development, and creating meaningful opportunities for young people to learn from and grow with one another.

A National Award Winner for his innovative work through Nyaaya Vaani, Pranav represents a new generation of young leaders who seek to combine innovation with social purpose, diplomacy with conviction, and leadership with service.

His distinctive identity as a "Diplomatic Authoritative Trail Blazer" reflects his approach to leadership—strategic in thought, authoritative in purpose, diplomatic in engagement, and pioneering in action.

For Pranav, youth leadership is not about creating followers; it is about creating leaders. Through IYC, he strives to build a culture of shared knowledge, peer mentorship, intellectual curiosity, civic responsibility, and collective growth.

Every initiative ultimately converges towards one larger national aspiration: Viksit Bharat. Guided by the spirit of Rashtra Devo Bhava and powered by Yuva Shakti, Pranav seeks to inspire young Indians to become not merely witnesses to India's transformation, but active architects of its future.

Ignite the Youth. Share Knowledge. Build Leaders. Serve the Nation.

Towards Viksit Bharat.

Suchindran Coimbatore Sankararaman

Founder & Visionary | SRESHTA Foundation

Suchindran Coimbatore Sankararaman is the Founder and visionary behind.

His vision for SRESHTA Foundation is to build a distinctive institutional platform that brings together professional consulting, education and training, entrepreneurship, sustainable agriculture and environmental initiatives, and youth development—creating meaningful intersections between economic opportunity and social impact.

With a strong belief in the transformative power of knowledge, Suchindran advocates an approach in which learning leads to capability, capability leads to enterprise, and enterprise contributes to sustainable development. His efforts are focused on creating accessible avenues for individuals, professionals, entrepreneurs, farmers and young people to acquire relevant knowledge, develop practical competencies and translate their potential into productive outcomes.

As an MSME, SRESHTA Foundation reflects his belief that smaller, agile institutions can become powerful catalysts for innovation and grassroots transformation. The Foundation seeks to operate at the intersection of enterprise and purpose, developing initiatives that are professionally structured, practically relevant and socially meaningful.

Suchindran's leadership philosophy is anchored in integrity, continuous learning, innovation, sustainability and responsible growth. He envisions SRESHTA Foundation not simply as an organisation delivering programmes and services, but as an evolving ecosystem that enables people to learn, create, lead and contribute.

Through SRESHTA Foundation, his larger aspiration is to foster a culture of self-reliance, entrepreneurship and responsible citizenship, while contributing to sustainable livelihoods, knowledge-driven growth and stronger communities.

Building Capability. Creating Opportunity. Advancing Sustainable Growth.

Under Suchindran's leadership, SRESHTA Foundation aspires to grow as a credible MSME institution that transforms ideas into initiatives, knowledge into capability and capability into lasting impact.
